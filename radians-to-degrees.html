    About Radian to Degree

    Engineers, scientists, and students often switch between degrees and radians. Our Radian → Degree converter handles it instantly.

    A full circle is 360 degrees = 2π radians. The conversion factor is exactly 180/π. We use the standard mathematical constant π = 3.14159265358979.

    FAQ — Radian to Degree

    Why do mathematicians use radians?

    Because sin(x) ≈ x is only true in radians. It's the "natural" unit for circles.

    How many degrees in π radians?

    Exactly 180. 2π rad = 360°.
