    About Week to Day

    Time conversions are sometimes surprising — they look easy but mistakes pile up. Our Week → Day converter makes sure every unit aligns.

    Time is the most internationally consistent unit. The second is the same on every continent. The year and month are approximations — this converter uses 365.25 days per year and 30.4375 days per month average.

    FAQ — Week to Day

    How many seconds in a day?

    Exactly 86,400 (24 × 60 × 60).

    How many days in a year?

    365 normal, 366 in a leap year. Average: 365.25 days = 31,557,600 seconds.

    How many weeks in a year?

    52 weeks + 1 or 2 days. Average: 52.18 weeks.
