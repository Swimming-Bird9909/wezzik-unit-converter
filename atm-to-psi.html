    FAQ — Atmosphere to PSI

    How many psi is 1 bar?

    1 bar = 14.5038 psi (exactly).

    Why is psi so different from kPa?

    1 psi = 6894.76 pascals. psi is older and still common in US industry; the SI standard is pascal/kPa.

    How do I read tire pressure units?

    Most US cars list psi (e.g. 32 psi cold). European and many new cars list bar (e.g. 2.2 bar). 1 bar ≈ 14.5 psi.

    Is 1 atm the same as 101.325 kPa?

    Yes — by definition 1 standard atmosphere = 101,325 Pa exactly.
