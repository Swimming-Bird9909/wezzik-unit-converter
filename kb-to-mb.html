    About Kilobyte to Megabyte

    Confused about whether a GB equals 1,000 or 1,024 MB? You're not alone — and your phone, Mac, Windows PC, and ISP can all show slightly different numbers for the same file. Our Kilobyte → Megabyte converter uses the SI standard by default.

    The SI standard (1 kB = 1000 B) is used by hard-drive makers, macOS, modern Windows, all networking, and the IEC. The old binary standard (1 KiB = 1024 B) is used inside RAM and was the original \"kilo\" in computing.

    FAQ — Kilobyte to Megabyte

    Is 1 kB 1000 bytes or 1024?

    By SI/IEC since 1998, 1 kB = 1000 bytes. The binary version is 1 KiB = 1024 bytes. Most modern systems use kB for 1000.

    Why does my 500 GB SSD show 465 GB?

    Because storage is sold in SI (500 × 1000 × 1000 × 1000 = 500 GB) but reported in binary by some tools (500 / 1.024^3 ≈ 465 GiB). Same drive, different counting.

    How many MB in a GB?

    1,000 MB (SI) or 1,024 MB (binary). This converter uses 1,000 by default.
