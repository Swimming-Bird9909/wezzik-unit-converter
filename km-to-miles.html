    How many km are in a mi?

    1 km = 0.621371 mi.

    Which is bigger, a km or a mi?

    A mi is approximately 1.6093 times bigger than a km.

    Is kilometer the same as mile?

    They measure the same kind of quantity (length), but they use different scales. Kilometer is metric; Mile is imperial.
