    About Gram to Cup (US)

    Grams to cup (us) conversions depend on the density of the ingredient. This converter is calibrated for all-purpose flour (1 cup ≈ 125 g); for other ingredients (e.g. flour, sugar, honey) the ratio differs.

    Volume-to-mass ratios like 125 g per cup are reference values widely used in cookbooks. They assume standard density at room temperature.

    FAQ — Gram to Cup (US)

    What's the difference between g and cup?

    G are metric units of mass (used worldwide in science and most countries); cup are imperial/U.S. customary, most commonly used for body weight in the US and UK.

    How many g in a cup?

    1 cup = 236.588 g.

    Is 1 lb exactly 0.4536 kg?

    It's exactly 0.45359237 kg by international agreement since 1959. We use that exact number in this converter.

    How do I quickly estimate without a calculator?

    Memorise: 1 kg ≈ 2.205 lb. To go from lb to kg, divide by 2.205. The chart above has the common values pre-calculated.

    What's the difference between mass and weight?

    In everyday language we use "weight" to mean mass. Technically weight is a force (depends on gravity), mass is the amount of matter. This converter treats them as the same thing — which is what every kitchen scale, body scale and shipping service does.
