    FAQ — Celsius to Kelvin

    How do I convert °C to K?

    Add 273.15: K = C + 273.15.

    At what temperature are °C and K the same?

    These scales never produce the same numerical value because their zero points differ.

    What's the boiling and freezing point in K?

    Water boils at 212 °F (100 °C), freezes at 32 °F (0 °C).

    Do I need to subtract 32 first or multiply first?

    Always subtract 32 first when converting °F to anything else. The formula is °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9.

    What is absolute zero in K?

    0 K (defined as exactly this).
