    FAQ — Inch to Millimeter

    How many in are in a mm?

    1 in = 25.4000 mm.

    Which is bigger, a in or a mm?

    A mm is approximately 0.0394 times bigger than a in.

    Is inch the same as millimeter?

    They measure the same kind of quantity (length), but they use different scales. Inch is metric; Millimeter is imperial.
