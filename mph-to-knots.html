    FAQ — Mile / hour to Knot

    How fast is 100 mph in km/h?

    100 mph = 160.934 km/h exactly.

    Is a knot the same as a nautical mile per hour?

    Yes, 1 knot = 1 nautical mile per hour = 1.852 km/h.

    How fast is the speed of sound?

    About 767 mph, 1235 km/h, or 343 m/s at sea level.
