Cookies Policy

This page explains what cookies are set when you use UnitWise and how to control them.

What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small text file placed on your device by a website. It allows the site to remember your actions and preferences over time.

NamePurposeTypeExpires
uw-themeRemembers dark/light mode preference.Functional1 year
uw-cookie-consentRemembers that you accepted the cookie banner.Functional1 year
uw-hist-*Stores your recent conversions locally.FunctionalIndefinite
GA / _ga / _gidAnonymized, aggregated usage analytics.Analytics13 months
Google AdSense cookiesPersonalized ads, frequency capping.AdvertisingUp to 2 years

Managing cookies

You can clear or block cookies in your browser settings at any time. Doing so won't prevent you from using UnitWise, but you'll see the cookie banner again and lose your saved theme.
