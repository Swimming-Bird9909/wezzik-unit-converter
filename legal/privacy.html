Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

UnitWise ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the rights you have over your data. By using our website you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide: When you contact us via email or a form, we receive whatever you choose to write to us.

Information collected automatically: When you visit UnitWise, our analytics provider (Google Analytics) collects anonymized usage data: pages visited, country/region, browser, device, and the URL you came from. We have enabled IP anonymization so your full IP address is not stored.

Local storage: We use your browser's localStorage to remember theme preference, recent conversion history, and cookie consent. We never transmit this data off your device.

2. Cookies

UnitWise uses the following cookies:

  • Functional cookies: theme, last-used converters — strictly necessary.
  • Analytics cookies: Google Analytics 4 cookies for anonymized, aggregated statistics.
  • Advertising cookies: Google AdSense cookies, set only after you accept the cookie banner.

3. Advertising

UnitWise displays advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google uses cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our site or other sites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the limited information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve UnitWise.
  • Understand aggregated usage patterns.
  • Respond to direct enquiries.
  • Detect and prevent abuse.

We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

5. Legal Bases (GDPR)

If you are in the EEA, our legal bases for processing are:

  • Consent for analytics and advertising cookies (you can withdraw it at any time via our cookie banner).
  • Legitimate interest for understanding aggregated, anonymized site usage.
  • Contract when you reach out to us — to respond.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal information. To exercise these rights, contact us via the address on our Contact page. We respond within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

UnitWise is intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

8. Changes to this Policy

We may update this policy. The "last updated" date at the top reflects the latest revision.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the email address listed on the Contact page.
