    Frequently asked questions

    Is UnitWise free?

    Yes — 100% free, no signup, no limits. We support it with unobtrusive ads.

    How accurate is the conversion?

    We use NIST-published factors with up to 8 significant digits. Temperature conversions use the standard formulas (e.g. °F = °C × 9/5 + 32).

    Why is 1 KB = 1000 B but my Mac says 1024?

    Industry adopted SI prefixes (1 KB = 1000 B) years ago. macOS uses binary GiB/KiB under the hood but displays them as GB/KB. Our converter defaults to the SI standard; the data storage page explains both.

    Can I convert between cooking ingredients?

    For cooking, mass-to-volume depends on the ingredient. We publish dedicated pages (grams of flour to cups, butter grams to tablespoons) with ingredient-specific factors.
