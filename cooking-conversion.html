Why cooking measurements are tricky

A "cup" is about 237 mL in the US, but ingredients vary in weight: one cup of flour is roughly 120 g, while one cup of sugar is about 200 g. That is why volume-to-weight conversions depend on the ingredient. UnitWise lists the common ones (flour, sugar, butter) as dedicated tools so you get a kitchen-accurate result instead of a rough average.

For best results, weigh ingredients with a kitchen scale — grams are far more consistent than cups. Use these converters when a recipe only gives you one system and you need the other.
