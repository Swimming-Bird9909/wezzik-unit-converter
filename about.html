About UnitWise

UnitWise is a free, fast, and accurate online unit converter built for everyday use — cooking, engineering, fitness, school, work, travel, and shopping.

What we built

UnitWise started with a single observation: most conversion tools online are bloated, slow, full of popups, or wrong. We wanted a tool that opens instantly, gives a correct answer, and doesn't ask us to sign up to view more than three results.

What we believe

  • Free forever. The core tools will always be free for everyone.
  • Privacy first. Math runs in your browser. We don't see your inputs.
  • Accuracy. We use NIST-accepted factors with up to 8 significant digits.
  • Speed. Pages load instantly. There's no framework overhead.

What we offer

  • 200+ conversion pages covering 13+ categories.
  • Real-time calculation as you type.
  • Quick-reference tables for every conversion.
  • Bookmark-friendly URLs.
  • Light and dark mode.

How we keep it running

The site is funded by unobtrusive advertising. We never require payment, account, or registration. If you'd like to support us further, sharing the tools helps more than anything.
