    About Liter to Pint (US)

    Volume conversions are among the most common on the internet — from kitchen recipes (cups vs milliliters), to fuel efficiency (gallons vs liters), to chemistry and pharmaceuticals. Our Liter → Pint (US) converter is built for exactly those everyday uses.

    Metric — both metric and US customary — use the same concept (3-D space occupied) but in different scales. The US customary system has been frozen since 1824. Importantly, a \"cup\" in Canada is 250 mL, but in the US it's 236.588 mL, which causes real-world mismatches in imported recipes. Our tool uses the US legal cup.

    FAQ — Liter to Pint (US)

    How many L in a pt?

    1 pt = 0.473176 L.

    Is 1 US gallon the same as 1 UK gallon?

    No. 1 US (liquid) gallon is 3.785 L; 1 UK (imperial) gallon is 4.546 L. This converter uses the US gallon unless otherwise noted.

    Are your fluid ounce conversions US or UK?

    Our fluid ounce is the US fluid ounce (29.5735 mL). The UK (imperial) fluid ounce is 28.4131 mL — slightly smaller.

    Why doesn't my metric cup match the recipe?

    Recipes from US sites assume 1 cup = 236.588 mL (US legal). Recipes from many other countries assume 250 mL. If you're following a US recipe, use the values from this converter.

    Is 100 mL the same as 100 cc?

    Almost. 1 mL = 1 cc (cubic centimeter). They're interchangeable in volume, though in medicine mL is preferred. This converter uses mL.
