    FAQ — Kilocalorie to Joule

    Is a food calorie the same as a physics calorie?

    No. 1 food Calorie (kcal) = 1000 small calories (cal).

    How many joules in a calorie?

    1 kcal = 4,184 J exactly. 1 cal = 4.184 J.

    How many kWh does an average home use?

    About 10,000 kWh per year in the US, much lower in Europe (≈3,000 kWh).
